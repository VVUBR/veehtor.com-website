Dragon Ball Hunt

A Dragon Ball Z × Age of Mythology skirmish. Seven Dragon Balls. One wish. Destroy the enemy Town Center to win.

Opponent
How to play

Goal

Destroy the enemy Town Center. Along the way, carry Dragon Balls (only heroes can) into your Temple. Each one trickles Ki and Zeni. Hold all seven and summon Shenron for a wish.

Mouse

  • Left-click or drag a box to select. Double-click selects every unit of that type on screen.
  • Right-click to command: move, attack, gather, build, carry a Dragon Ball, deposit it at your Temple.
  • Arrow keys or screen edges scroll; mouse wheel zooms; click the minimap to jump.
  • Keys: H hero, M army, I idle villager, A attack-move, P pause, Esc cancel. Command buttons show their key.

Touch

  • Tap your unit to select it. With units selected, tap the ground, an enemy or a resource to give the order.
  • Drag to scroll, pinch to zoom. Turn on Box select to drag-select a group.

Combat rules

  • Heroes deal 5× damage to myth units (2.5× to Titans). Myth units deal 3× to human soldiers and knock them back, but only 0.25× to heroes. Human soldiers deal 1.25× to heroes.
  • Flying costs 0.5 Ki per second per fighter. At 0 Ki everyone drops to the ground.
